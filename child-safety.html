MapFrens – Child Safety Standards

MapFrens is committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

Age restriction

MapFrens is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly allow children to use the app.

Prohibited content

MapFrens strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation of minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or inappropriate interactions involving minors

Any such content or behaviour is not permitted on the platform.

User safety controls

To help maintain a safe environment, MapFrens provides:

  • User blocking features
  • Account deletion controls
  • Restricted one-to-one interactions only
  • No public content posting

Reporting and enforcement

If we become aware of content or behaviour involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, we take appropriate action, including:

  • Removing offending accounts and content
  • Reporting to relevant regional and national authorities, as required by law
