Mapfrens – Account and Data Deletion

Information for Mapfrens Android App users on Google Play

How to Delete Your Account

Deleting your Mapfrens account is simple and can be done directly within the app. No email, manual approval, or external request is required.

📍 Navigate to: Settings → Delete Account

Once you initiate the deletion from this menu, your account will be processed for removal.

What Data is Deleted

When you delete your Mapfrens account, the following data is deleted:

  • User profile information – Your account details and profile data
  • Google authentication identifier – Your Google sign-in credentials associated with the app
  • Chat messages – All conversations and message history
  • Location-related data – Your location sharing history and location records
  • App activity data – Records of your interactions within the app

Data Retention Policy

After you delete your account, please note the following:

Temporary Retention

Some data may be retained for up to 30 days after account deletion. This retention occurs only for the following purposes:

  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Protection against abuse and misuse

Permanent Deletion

After the 30-day retention period, all retained data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Partial Data Deletion

⚠️ Partial data deletion is not supported in Mapfrens. To delete associated data, you must delete your entire account. Individual data types (such as messages or location history alone) cannot be deleted separately.

Questions or Issues?

If you have any questions about account deletion or data privacy, please refer to our Privacy Policy or Terms and Conditions.
